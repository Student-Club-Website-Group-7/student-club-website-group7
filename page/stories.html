2005

Where It All Started

PNC Cambodia Begins

Passerelles Numériques Cambodia (PNC) began its activities in Phnom Penh in 2005, providing IT training and supporting students not only in technical skills but also in professional skills, personal development, and well-being.

2015

Students Find Their
Voice

The First Student Association

In 2015, PNC created its first Student Association. The initiative encouraged students to develop responsibility, independence, initiative, and leadership while becoming more involved in the PNC community.

Clubs Grow With
Students

11 Student Clubs

By 2019, PNC documented 11 Student Clubs created around students' interests. These included Library, Painting, Newspaper, Solidarity, Dancing, Singing, Theater, Football, Cooking, and Maintenance Clubs.
